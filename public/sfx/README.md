# sfx/

All audio here is AI-generated with Suno (Pro plan, commercial licence) on 2026-11-02, then trimmed and loudness-normalised with ffmpeg by Claude. Nothing is sampled from anywhere else.

- `theme_{m|f}_{zh|en}.mp3` — the theme song 《今天不可以》 / *Not Today*: words by the author (好大壮), English words Claude, music and voice Suno v6 (covers of one v6-mini take, so every version shares the melody). Heard on the title page, the ending credits, and the walkman on the desk after the ending.
- `music-day.mp3`, `music-night.mp3`, `music-dream.mp3` — instrumental variations of the theme (Suno v6 covers, `[Instrumental]`), the day/night tracks and the one for the three dreams.
- `bed-room-day.mp3`, `bed-room-night.mp3`, `bed-room-deep.mp3` — room-tone loops (Suno Sounds, loop type), 13–18 s, seamless.
- `sting-soft.mp3`, `sting-hard.mp3` — night-beat stingers (Suno Sounds, one-shot).
- `doorbell`, `pager-buzz`, `chick-beep`, `lamp-click`, `lock`, `chain`, `key-press` — one-shots (Suno Sounds), replacing the earlier ffmpeg-synthesised placeholders.
- `rain-loop.mp3` — still the ffmpeg placeholder; swap when a better one exists.

Missing files are silent by design (see `.kiro/specs/sound/requirements.md`).
