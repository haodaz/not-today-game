01 · Bottleneck

    
Everyone is making AI bigger, and in the end it is stuck on people: a brain weaker than the AI is steering the AI

    
Take today's AI and the brain apart side by side: the brain has about 86 billion neurons[1] on about 20 watts[2]; it is divided into regions[4], lights only a small patch at a time[3], learns while it works[5][6], and gets it from a few examples[7]. Today's AI is one block: computes everything, freezes once trained, needs oceans of samples, and megawatt-hours to learn[8]. One block also means when it goes wrong, nobody can stop it.
