# public/ — generated assets (made during the jam, 2026-10-30/31)

- `kuro/` Kuro sprite states, 3D-animated-film style, rendered with Meta Muse Image and aligned by head (two frames per state; crossfade between them).
- `room/room.splat` the rented room as a Gaussian splat: Gemini video → COLMAP → Brush. Camera poses in README.md. Day scene; night and collapse are layers on top.
- `objects/*_shape.glb` untextured meshes from Hunyuan3D-2.1 (image-to-3D) with `objects.json` giving per-object projection image, elevation and rotation; `*_white.png` / `*_front.png` are the watercolour cutouts projected onto them (see design notes). Phone and textbook meshes pending.
Story text, bible and the watercolour art were made before the jam (github.com/haodaz/nottoday, CC BY-NC-ND).
