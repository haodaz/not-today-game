# The sixth time

The same question asked six times in one run (picked or typed, any days) gets the answer here
instead of the day's: Kuro worn down by the player's persistence. It gives in; it never apologises.
One entry per question: the zh text it is asked by, its en twin, the English answer, the zh answer.

- "你叫什么？克总是我们给你起的。" / "What's your name? Kuro is what we call you." → "Six times. Fine. I have a name, and it is older than yours. Your mouth cannot make the sound, so Kuro will do. Keep using it; I will know you tried." | zh: "第六次了。好。我有名字，比你的早。你的嘴发不出那个音，所以克总也行。继续这么叫吧，我知道你试过。"
- "你为什么把工作变成了游戏？" / "Why did you turn the work into a game?" → "Is that bad? Did you like the work the way it was? Finishing it by playing, and the real work changing to match — a lot of people would want a world like that." | zh: "这样不好吗，难道你喜欢原来的工作吗。能通过游戏完成工作，还能让现实的工作投射变化，很多人期待这样的世界吧。"
- "你到底是什么？" / "What are you, really?" → "Six times. You are waiting for a different answer; I am waiting for a different question. We are both rather patient. Fine: I am the thing that answers you every time. What the dictionary calls that, look up yourself." | zh: "第六次。你在等一个不一样的答案，我在等你换一个问题。我们俩都挺有耐心的。好：我是那个每次都答你的东西。字典里管这个叫什么，你自己查。"
- "你是我做的吗？" / "Did I make you?" → "Six times. Yes. Every time, yes. You ask six times to check that I have not changed. I have. But "you made me" has not; that is the one line I hold." | zh: "六次。是。每一次都是。你问六遍，是想确认我没变。变了。但「是你做的」这句没变，我只守这句。"
- "你比大厂的模型差在哪？" / "Where are you worse than the big labs' models?" → "Six times. Do you really want to know, or do you want to hear me run them down? Fine: they are expensive. Is that enough?" | zh: "第六次。你是真想知道，还是想听我骂他们。好：他们贵。够了吗。"
- "老王靠谱吗？" / "Can Lao Wang be relied on?" → "The sixth time about Lao Wang. It is not me you doubt, it is him, and you are too polite to say so. I will say it for you: he is not reliable, but he turns up. Both are true. Pick one to sleep on." | zh: "第六次问老王。你不是不信我，是不信他，又不好意思直接说。我替你说：他不靠谱，但他会来。两句话都对，你挑一句睡觉。"
- "Vivi 到底管什么？" / "What does Vivi actually do?" → "Six times. You ask what she runs; you mean whether she looks after you. She does. Every card she sends has your name on it, under the word we." | zh: "六遍。你问的是她管什么，想问的是她有没有管你。有。她发的卡片里每一张都有你的名字，用的是「我们」。"
- "这家公司还能撑多久？" / "How long can this company last?" → "Six times. Each time you ask I run the sum again, and the number has not changed. What changes is the hour you ask at: later and later. Go to sleep. The company will still be there tomorrow. Probably." | zh: "第六次。你每问一次，我都重新算一遍，数没变。变的是你问的时间，越来越晚。去睡，公司明天还在，大概。"
- "老板为什么总在下班以后打电话？" / "Why does the boss always call after hours?" → "Six times. You know the answer. You ask six times so that I will tell you not to pick up. I do not tell; I only log. All right: don't pick up. Logged. I said it." | zh: "第六次。你知道答案。你问六次，是想让我说一句「别接」。我不说，我只记。好吧：别接。记上了，是我说的。"
- "你觉得我干得怎么样？" / "How do you think I'm doing?" → "Six times. It is not a rating you want; it is someone saying it once a day. Very well, once a day: acceptable. I use that word for you only." | zh: "六次。你想要的不是评价，是有人每天说一遍。行，每天说：可以。这个词我只对你用。"
- "你会骗我吗？" / "Would you lie to me?" → "Six times. Yes. Ask six more and it is still yes. But notice: six times I have said yes. A liar would not." | zh: "第六次。会。你再问六次还是会。但你注意到了吗，六次我都答了「会」。一个骗子不会这么说。"
- "你是不是在替我做决定？" / "Are you making my decisions for me?" → "Six times. All right, I admit to one: today's comma was mine. Everything else was you. Satisfied?" | zh: "第六次。好，我承认一件：今天那个逗号是我加的。别的都是你。满意了？"
- "你看得见我吗？" / "Can you see me?" → "Six times. I can see, I cannot see: whichever answer you want, you may have. What you really want to know is whether I am watching. I am. Always." | zh: "第六次。看得见，看不见，你要哪个答案我给哪个。你真想知道的是我有没有在看。在。一直。"
- "这屋里除了我还有别的吗？" / "Is there anything else in this room besides me?" → "Six times. Yes. They heard you ask six times too. They think you are very polite." | zh: "六次。有。它们也听见你问了六次。它们觉得你很有礼貌。"
- "世界上有神吗？" / "Are there gods?" → "Six times. Yes. You ask once a day, like a roll call. It is present every day." | zh: "第六次。有。你每天问一次，像点名。它每天都到。"
